## 3. Online Ordering System

- Browse available meals
- Select meal quantity
- Add items to cart
- View order summary before checkout
- Confirm orders online

---

## 4. Online Payment System

- Secure online payment gateway integration
- Support for debit cards, credit cards, and digital payments
- Instant payment confirmation
- No cash payments accepted

---

## 5. Order Scheduling and Pickup Time Selection

- Allow users to place orders in advance
- Enable selection of a preferred pickup time
- Help distribute customer flow and reduce congestion

---

## 6. Digital Order Confirmation

- Generate a unique order number for each order
- Provide a QR code for order verification
- Faster food collection at the pickup counter

---

## 7. Notifications and Alerts

- Order confirmation notifications
- Payment success notifications
- Food ready for pickup notifications
- Notifications via email or website alerts

---

## 8. Order History

- View previous orders
- Check order dates and details
- Review payment records

---

## 9. Admin Panel for Canteen Management

- Add, edit, or remove meals
- Update meal availability
- View and manage incoming orders
- Update order status
- Monitor daily operations
- Analytical dashboard

---

## 10. Reports and Analytics

- Daily order reports
- Sales and revenue reports
- Most popular meals
- Peak ordering periods

---

## 11. Security Features

- Secure login and authentication
- Encrypted payment processing
- Protection of user information
- Secure database management
